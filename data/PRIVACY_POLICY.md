# Privacy Policy

**App:** SpaceSlim
**Last updated:** August 6, 2026

SpaceSlim ("the App", "we", "our") is a storage‑cleaning utility that helps you
free up space on your device by compressing videos, finding removable photos
(similar, duplicate, blurry), and hiding sensitive media in an encrypted vault.

Your privacy is the foundation of this app: **SpaceSlim runs entirely on your
device. We do not operate servers, we have no user accounts, and we never upload,
collect, sell, or share your photos, videos, or personal data.**

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect personal information (name, email, phone number, address).
- We do **not** upload your photos, videos, or their metadata anywhere.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, or cookies.
- We do **not** create accounts or profiles.
- We do **not** sell or share any data with third parties, because we do not
  collect any to begin with.

## 2. On‑Device Access (What the App Uses, Locally)

To do its job, the App requests access to the following, and everything is
processed **only on your device**:

- **Photo Library (read & write):** Used to scan and classify your media
  (similar/duplicate/blurry photos, large videos, screenshots, screen
  recordings, portraits), to compress videos, and to delete or move items that
  **you** choose. Photo analysis (including on‑device machine learning such as
  Apple's Vision framework for image similarity and face detection) happens
  locally; no image ever leaves your device.
- **Device Storage Information:** Used to display how full your device is and how
  much space can be reclaimed. This information is read locally and is not
  transmitted.
- **Temporary Files:** During video compression, the App writes temporary files
  to your device's temporary storage and deletes them after the operation
  completes. You can also clear leftover temporary files from Settings.

## 3. Deleting and Compressing Media

- **Deletions** always go through the operating system's own confirmation
  prompt. Removed items are placed in the system's **"Recently Deleted"** album
  and can be recovered there for 30 days before permanent removal, per your
  operating system's behavior.
- **Compression** creates a new, smaller copy of a video. If you enable "Replace
  originals," the original is deleted (through the system confirmation) after the
  compressed copy is saved. You are always in control of these actions.

## 4. Private Vault

The Private Vault lets you move photos and videos out of the system Photos app
and into a hidden, protected area inside the App.

- Items you add to the Vault are **encrypted at rest on your device** using
  AES‑256 (GCM). The encryption key is generated on your device and stored in
  the device **Keychain**; it never leaves your device.
- When you move an item into the Vault, the original is removed from the system
  Photos app (through the system confirmation) so it is no longer visible there.
- The Vault is protected by **Face ID / Touch ID / device passcode**
  (via Apple's LocalAuthentication). We do not have, store, or transmit your
  biometric data — authentication is handled entirely by the operating system.
- Vault contents are stored only on your device and are excluded from iCloud
  backups. **Because there is no cloud copy, if you delete the App or lose your
  device, vaulted items cannot be recovered by us.** You can restore items back
  to Photos at any time from within the Vault.

## 5. Network Use

SpaceSlim does not require an internet connection for its core features and does
not send your content over the network. Any network access permitted by the
operating system (for example, to download a photo that is stored only in iCloud
so it can be processed locally) is initiated solely to complete an action you
requested, and the retrieved content is processed on your device only.

## 6. Children's Privacy

SpaceSlim does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children. Because we collect no data at all, the App is safe to use
with respect to children's privacy.

## 7. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security is protected by your
device's own security model (device passcode, encryption, and sandboxing).
Vaulted media is additionally encrypted by the App as described above. Please
keep your device passcode and biometrics secure.

## 8. Your Choices and Control

- You may grant, limit, or revoke photo access at any time in the system
  Settings app.
- You choose what is compressed, deleted, hidden, or restored.
- You may clear temporary files at any time from the App's Settings.
- You may remove the App at any time; doing so removes all of its on‑device data,
  including Vault contents.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at
the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after an
